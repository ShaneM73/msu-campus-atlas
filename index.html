Missouri State University

A public university of nearly 27,000 students, built around a 225-acre campus a mile from downtown Springfield. It started as a teachers' school with 543 students and now offers more than 170 degree programs.

01 · Location

A campus in the middle of the city

The Springfield campus sits about a mile southeast of Park Central Square, inside Elm Street, Grand Street, National Avenue and Kimbrough Avenue. Two university sites sit downtown: the Jordan Valley Innovation Center and Brick City.
